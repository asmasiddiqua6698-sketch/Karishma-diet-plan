How was your stomach, sleep, stress and energy? Two lines is enough. This helps us find your triggers.

Daily rules

  • Do not go more than 3.5 hours without food on teaching days.
  • Eat slowly, sit upright for 30 minutes after meals, finish dinner 2.5 to 3 hours before sleep.
  • Tea: up to 2 cups, after food, never on an empty stomach.
  • Fried snacks (samosa, puff) once a week at most.
  • On stressful days when appetite drops, a shake or a handful of nuts and dates still counts.
  • Sip water through the day, about 2 to 2.5 litres.

Weekly menu

A 7-day cycle that repeats every week. Every meal gives 15 to 20 g protein with healthy fats and slow carbohydrates. Swap any two days if needed. Soak dals, millets and oats overnight and cook them soft.

Protein powder guide

  • Choose a whey isolate (vegetarian, lower in lactose) or, if milk causes bloating, a pea and rice blend. Pick a lab-tested brand with no added sugar.
  • Days 1 to 4: a quarter scoop in the mid-morning shake, never on an empty stomach.
  • From day 5: half a scoop in the mid-morning shake. On strength days (Mon, Wed, Fri) you may add a second half scoop after the session.
  • Aim for about two thirds of your protein from food. Powder fills the gap and does not replace meals.
  • If you feel gas, acidity or heaviness, reduce to half a scoop and tell us.

Strength and movement

Building muscle needs resistance training and enough food. Your daily steps and classes already burn a lot, so the plan adds strength sessions and trims extra walking.

Weekly shape
  • Strength: 3 sessions a week, 30 to 40 minutes, with a rest day between.
  • Kathak twice a week continues. It counts as your cardio.
  • Steps: about 8,000 to 10,000 on teaching days. Skip the 20,000 days.
  • One complete rest day a week.
Session A
  • Goblet squat or sit to stand: 3 x 10
  • Glute bridge: 3 x 12
  • Incline push up: 3 x 8 to 10
  • Band or dumbbell row: 3 x 10
  • Plank: 3 x 20 to 30 seconds
Session B
  • Romanian deadlift, light dumbbells: 3 x 10
  • Split squat: 3 x 8 each side
  • Shoulder press: 3 x 10
  • Band pull apart or lat pulldown: 3 x 12
  • Dead bug: 3 x 8 each side
Before and after
  • Before: a banana with milk or toast 45 to 60 minutes earlier. Do not train on an empty stomach.
  • After: a shake or a full meal within 1 hour.
  • Start light, learn the form first, and add weight when the last set feels easy. A trainer should check your form in the first week.
  • Sleep 7 to 8 hours. Muscle is built during recovery.

Progress log

Weigh once a week, same morning, same conditions. Check body composition once a month on the same device.

Tests to complete with your doctor

  • Iron studies with ferritin, vitamin B12 and folate
  • Repeat fasting blood sugar with fasting insulin, after 10 to 12 hours of fasting
  • Repeat urine test (mid stream sample) and urine culture if advised
  • Celiac screen with total IgA, if your gastroenterologist agrees

Also tell your doctor if you are still taking pantoprazole, and for how long.

Contact your doctor promptly if

  • Black, tarry or bloody stool
  • Vomiting that does not settle, or trouble swallowing
  • Fainting, severe stomach pain, or ongoing weight loss
  • Missed or very late periods
